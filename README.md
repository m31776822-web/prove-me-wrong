# Prove Me Wrong

Sit across from Charlie Kirk. Pick a topic. Make your case.

This is an AI campus-table debate, not a seance. The persona is distilled from the open-source skill:

**[YixiaJack/charlie-kirk-skill](https://github.com/YixiaJack/charlie-kirk-skill)** (MIT)

Grounded in public speeches, Turning Point USA campus debates, *The Charlie Kirk Show*, *The MAGA Doctrine* (2020), and *The College Scam* (2022). Information cutoff for his views: September 2025.

## How it plays

1. Choose a claim — free college, guns, DEI, borders, speech, wages — or write your own.
2. He opens. You answer. Up to six rounds.
3. Call the debate. An independent judge scores both sides.
